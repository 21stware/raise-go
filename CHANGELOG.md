## 0.7.1 — 2026-11-01

- `k3`: `ViaOpen` (`"open"`), `AssertionClaims.Scopes` and `OpenInput` (ADR-0130 P11 S3): the platform forwards a call of its open API (`/open/v1/…`) to the App that answers it with an assertion of via `open` whose subject is who the call is for (`customer:{id}` for a customer-mode token), whose `scopes` are what the caller's token was granted and whose input digest binds the request (`OpenInput`: method, path, raw query, JSON body or null). The caller's own token and credential never reach the App.
- `appkit`: `Inbound` accepts the `open` assertion on `/k3/open/v1/…` only, and nothing else there; one without a subject, or for another request, is refused (401). `Platform.Scopes` carries the granted scopes (`PlatformOf`); the app's route checks the scope it needs.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-11-01

- `appkit` (ADR-0130 P11 S3, as the platform's own app runner `internal/remoteapp`): only the app's own call goes with its tenant token, and the app's own call never carries the person's page session. `Client.App` and `Tokens.App` name the app the client calls for (set both to the manifest's id). A caller with no subject, or exactly `"app:"+App`, is the app's own call: its tenant token, even inside a person's request (before, `Tokens.Source` handed out the person's page session for any caller while one was on the context); a person's call keeps the page session; any other `"app:…"` subject (another app's, a prefix match, or any while `App` is empty) is refused with `ErrForbidden` before a token is asked for. No request makes such a subject a person: `Inbound` refuses an App token whose subject starts with `app:` (401), a platform assertion naming one leaves `Platform.Caller` zero, the callers of the signed deal, grant and hold-released bodies that name one reach the app with no subject, and an op is refused (401) for an `app:` subject. An app that calls with its own `app:{id}` subject must set `Client.App` and `Tokens.App`.
- `k3`: `Permission.Defaults` (default holders of an app's point that do not gate its listing; ADR-0130 P8 S3), `ResourceDecl.Rules` with `ResourceRulesApp` (a flat type whose rules the app keeps) and `ProvidersDecl.AccessRequests` (`providers.accessRequests`: the app answers `levels`, `deciders` and `grant` for access requests on its own resources).
- `Client.App` (generated): the kernel's access requests: `Resources.PendingRequests`, `MyRequests`, `ApproveRequest`, `DenyRequest`, `CancelRequest`.
- `appkit`: what an app that keeps documents needs, on top of 0.6.0's org chart, backlinks provider and outbox (ADR-0130 P8 S3, first user: `raise-knowledge`): `Client.Realtime(manifest)` (`RealtimeService`, the platform's real-time service over `/app/v1/realtime`: Hubs of the manifest's `realtime.docs[]`, heads, projections, `ReadMeta`), `Client.Tree()` (the access kernel's tree, `access.Tree`), `Client.Backlinks` (`GET /app/v1/backlinks`, the other apps' records that mention one of the app's own); `Deps.Tree`, `Deps.Realtime`, `Deps.BacklinksAPI`, `Deps.Changes` (`TenantChanges`); `Deps.Files(app)` (a `blobs.Store` over `Deps.Blobs` in the app's folder, `FilesPrefix`), `Deps.BacklinksOf`, `Deps.Enqueue`, `IsDry`, `StoreWriteError`; the outbox delivers the app's own kinds besides `ledger.post`, `deal.handover` and the calendar pushes (`RegisterOutboxHandler`, `OutboxHandlerFunc`, `OutboxRow`, `OutboxResult`, `OutDone` / `OutSkipped` / `OutFailed` / `OutRetry`); `Serve` answers the providers `retrieve` and `embeddings` (`Retrieving`, `Embedding`; `PathRetrieve`, `PathEmbeddingDocs`, `PathEmbeddingChunks`, `EmbeddingPage`) and the services of `realtime.doc.projected` (`RealtimeProjecting`), `app.installed` and `permissions.changed` (`EventAppInstalled`, `EventPermissionsChanged`, fed to `Deps.Changes`; a service the app answers itself through `AppEvents` is left to it).
- `appkit`: access requests on the app's own resources (ADR-0130 P8, same names and JSON as the platform's `firstparty` / `providers`, round-tripped against them in `internal/remoteapp`): `AccessRequests` (`Levels`, `Deciders`, `Grant`), `AccessLevels`, `AccessGrant`, `AccessRequestHost`, `AccessRequestsCall`, `PathAccessRequests`, `AccessRequestsTimeout`, `AccessOpLevels` / `AccessOpDeciders` / `AccessOpGrant`; `Serve` answers `POST /k3/providers/access-requests` (levels and grant for the asserted person, deciders as the platform; a refusal is the app's error with its status) for an app that implements `AccessRequestHost` and declares `providers.accessRequests`. `Client.AccessRequests` and `Deps.AccessRequestsAPI` forward one of the app's own access-request routes to the kernel's `/app/v1/access/…` with the person's token, the answer's status and body as they are.
- `realtime`, `realtime/pmdoc`, `access`, `blobs`, `textsearch`: the SDK packages an app needs for real-time documents, the access kernel's tree, its files and keyword search, copied from the platform by `tools/raisego` (as `k3` and `provide`; ADR-0130 P8 S3, first user: `raise-knowledge`).

## 0.6.0 — 2026-11-01

- `Client.App` (generated): the `/app/v1` routes added since 0.5.0: `Deals.Emit`; `Org.Members`; `Peers` (`Desk`, `DeskHttp`, `Till`, `TillHttp`, `Holdable`, `Holds`, `Hold`, `HoldsFree`, `HoldsMove`, `HoldsRelease`, `ReferralCodes`, `ReferralCode`, `ReferralPromoter`); `Realtime` (`GetState`, `GetProjection`, `PublishTopic`, `CloseConnections`, `InvalidateConnections`, `InvalidateDocs`, `RevokeDocs`, `ScrubDoc`); `Resources.Naming` and `Resources.Depart` (a flat type's grants naming a person, and a departure; the app's own token only).
- `provide` / `appkit` (ADR-0130 P11 S2): system tables (`providers.tables`). The contract moved into `provide` (`TableProvider`, `TableCaller`, `TableSpec`, `TableField`, `TableQuery`, `RowWrite`, `ObjectRecord`, `ObjectFilter`, `ObjectPage`, `ObjectFieldDef`, `ObjectProblem`, `ObjectError`, `SystemTablePrefix`, `TableInsert` / `TableUpdate` / `TableDelete`, `TableViaStaff` / `TableViaVisitor` / `TableViaIntegration`, `TableRefused`, `TableNotWritable`, `TableInvalid`); the platform's `firstparty` names are aliases of them. `appkit` re-exports them, adds `TableProviding` and the `PathTable*` constants, and `Serve` answers `/k3/providers/tables/{specs,list,get,write}` for an app that implements it and declares `providers.tables`. `TableQuery` and `RowWrite` now have JSON names (`filters`, `ids`, `sort`, `limit`, `cursor`, `offset`; `op`, `id`, `values`, `version`).
- `appkit` (ADR-0130 P11 S3, what the rehab apps of `raise-rehab` need; same names and JSON as the platform's `firstparty`, each round-tripped against the platform's own client of the route in `internal/remoteapp`). Nothing existing changes signature: the interfaces other repositories implement (`CustomerClient`, `EntitlementClient`, `Approvals`) stay as they are, and the wider calls are on new interfaces.
  - Customers: `CustomerCentre` (`CustomerClient` + `Find`, `List`, `Timeline`, `Rename`, `Link`, `SetOwner`, `MarkDealt`, `Merge`, `Erase`; `CustomerQuery`, `CustomerPage`, `CustomerTouch`), `Client.CustomerCentre()`, `Deps.CustomerCentre()`.
  - Entitlements: `EntitlementCentre` (`EntitlementClient` + `ByCode`, `Templates`, `GranterNames`, `GranterChoices`, `GranterRefund`; `TemplateFilter`, `GranterChoice`, `GranterRefund`, `GranterRefundResult`, `ErrNotRefundable`), `Client.EntitlementCentre()`, `Deps.EntitlementCentre()`.
  - `Client.Org()` (`OrgChart`, `OrgUnit`, `OrgRole`; `Deps.Org`), `Client.Flat()` (`FlatGrants` with `Naming` and `Depart`, `FlatGrant`, `FlatSubject`; `Deps.Flat`), `Client.Peers()` (`Peers`: the other apps' desk, till, holds and promotion codes over `/app/v1/peers`, the desk and till requests handed over as `HTTPCall` / `HTTPAnswer`; `Deps.PeersAPI`, `Deps.Peers()`, `ErrNoResourceApp`).
  - Deal events of an app on its own: `Client.Deals` (`POST /app/v1/deals`), `Deps.DealsAPI`, `Deps.EnqueueDeal` (one `deal.handover` row in the app's outbox, `OutboxDealHandover`, `DealHandoverKey`), `Deps.SellsDeals`; the outbox delivers it (`Outbox.DealsAPI`).
  - Calendar pushes in the app's own transaction: `Deps.CalendarUpsert` / `CalendarCancel` queue `calendar.event.upsert` / `.cancel` rows (`OutboxCalendarUpsert`, `OutboxCalendarCancel`, the privacy constants `CalendarNormal` / `CalendarPrivate` / `CalendarHealth`), which the outbox hands to `Deps.CalendarAPI` (`Client.CalendarPush`; `Outbox.CalendarAPI`).
  - `Serve` answers the provider kinds `customerAssert`, `people`, `backlinks` (+ count), `holdings`, `agenda`, `work`, `desk`, `till`, `handover`, `holds`, `holdWatch` (the `...ing` interfaces of `providers.go`, method names as the platform's apps have them; `Path*` constants; `ErrNoCard`, `ErrHoldConflict`, `ErrBadPhone`, `AmbiguousCustomerError`), the Granters of `entitlements.grantable` (`Granting`, `Granter`, `GranterCatalog`, `GranterRefunder`), the hosted approvals' PREPARE and DECIDED (`ApprovalAnswering`, `ApprovalAnswerer`) and the payment service (`Settling`, `Settler`, `PaymentCall`, `PaymentService()`).
  - Fixes found by the round trips: `Approvals.Waiting` sends its limit and an app's own approval call (no person) goes with the app's token inside a person's request; a blob key with an empty, `.` or `..` segment is `ErrInvalid` before anything goes out; a delegated draft actor is refused (403) instead of reading as the person; a payment refusal keeps what it stands for (`ErrPayPending`, `ErrPayNotConfigured`, `ErrPayDisabled`, `PayGatewayError`) next to the kit's error of its status.
- `appkit`: `HTTPError.RetryAfter` (`time.Duration`): the platform's `Retry-After` of a refusal (a 429 of its rate limits, a 503), read from the answer by every `/app/v1` client, in delay-seconds or an HTTP date; 0 when the platform sent none. An app that retries waits at least that long (the journey app of `raise-growth` defers a rate-limited step by it).

## 0.5.0 — 2026-11-01

- `Client.App.Links.Backlinks`: `GET /backlinks?ref=` lists the other apps' records that mention one of the calling app's own records, as the page session's person sees them (`AppLinksBacklinksParams`, `AppBacklinkList`, `AppBacklink`; scope `app.links:read`, the app's own call gets none).
- `Client.App.Realtime.ReadCodecMeta`: `POST /realtime/codecs/{codec}/meta` reads an Office state's properties (title, the hashes of the file's preserved parts, the engine) with the platform's codec, for an app that checks an import outside the platform (`AppRealtimeCodecMetaRequest`, `AppRealtimeCodecMeta`). A topic `<type>:*` (the list of a declared type) may be published and subscribed by whoever holds the kind's admit point.
- `k3`: `Service.Schedule` (`Schedule{Cron, Timezone, Missed}`, `ParseCron`, `Cron.Next`, `ScheduleInput`, `ScheduleKey`, `MissedSkip` / `MissedOnce`, `DefaultScheduleZone`, `ScheduleGrace`): a service with the `schedule` trigger says when the platform starts its runs; the manifest is refused without it.
- `k3`: `events.emits` is checked (the app's own `{id}.{what}`, not one of `PlatformEventKinds`, at most `MaxEmits`); `Manifest.EmitsKind`.
- `appkit`: a customer's reach: `Client.Reach()` (`Reach` with `History`, `Reachable`; `SentMessage`, `Reachability`), `ScopeOutreachReach`. The generated client has `App.Outreach.CustomerMessages`, `Reachable`.
- `appkit`: customer tags: `Client.CustomerTags()` (`CustomerTags` with `Tags`, `Tag`; `CustomerTag`), `ScopeCustomersTag`. The generated client has `App.Customers.ListTags`, `AddTag`, `RemoveTag`.
- `appkit`: consent changes: `ConsentService(id, title)`, `ConsentHandling` (`ConsentChanges(d) ConsentFunc`), `ConsentChange`, `KindConsentChanged`, `ScopeConsentEvents` (the manifest must use it), served by `Serve` behind the assertion.
- `appkit`: events between apps: `Client.Emit` (`Emit`) publishes a declared kind; `EventService(id, title, kinds...)` declares a subscriber, and an app that implements `AppEventHandling` (`AppEvents(d) map[string]AppEventFunc`, `AppEvent`) is called by `Serve` behind the assertion.
- `appkit`: `ScheduleService(id, title, cron, zone, missed)` declares one; an app that implements `Scheduling` (`Schedules(d) map[string]ScheduleFunc`) is called at its endpoint by `Serve` for every time of the schedule, per tenant, with the platform's assertion checked.
- `Client.App`: header parameters of the spec are generated as call options. `WithDryRun()` sends `K3-Dry-Run: 1` (a dry run: the platform answers as if done and keeps nothing) on the operations that take it, e.g. `c.App.Entitlements.CreateTemplate(ctx, body, raise.WithDryRun())`. Before, the generated client could not send it.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-31

- `k3`: the manifest, the platform's assertions and keys (generated from the platform's protocol package by `tools/raisego`).
- `provide`: what an app answers when the platform asks (the provider contracts of `k3.ProvidersDecl`), generated the same way.
- `appkit` (v0, may change): the app side of the platform contract for an app server in its own repository — router, `Caller` from the platform's App token, ops and drafts, tenant-scoped `Tx`, the ledger outbox, deal events, site actions, data events (`tenant.deleted`, `site.deleted`), `Serve` for the providers a manifest declares, and `Client` for `/app/v1`. Standard library only; the app picks its SQL driver. First user: `raise-distribution` (ADR-0130 P4).
- `appkit`: the new `/app/v1` clients: `Client.Blobs(tenant)` (`BlobStore`, `ObjectInfo`, `ErrObjectNotFound`), `Client.Notify` with `WithNoticeClass` / `NoticeClass` and `Deps.Tell`, `Client.Drafts()` (a `DraftService` over HTTP, `DraftError`, `Create`) and `Client.Outreach()` (`OutreachClient`); `Deps.Blobs`, `Deps.Notify`, `Deps.OutreachAPI`.
- `appkit`: more `/app/v1` clients: `Client.Options()` (`OptionStore`, `OptionList`, `OptionsChange`; dry run by `K3-Dry-Run`), `Client.Model()` (`Model`, `ModelClient`, `ErrModelOff`), `Client.Pay()` (`Pay`, `PayOrder`, `PayRefund`, `PayCreateReq`, `PayRefundReq`), `Client.Approvals()` (`Approvals`, over the `k3` approval types) and `Client.CalendarPush` (`CalendarProjection`); `Deps.OptionsAPI`, `Deps.Model`, `Deps.Pay`, `Deps.Approvals`. The outbox has no handler registry yet, so a calendar push is not queued in the app's transaction.
- `Client.App.Resources`: the access kernel's tree model on the same routes (a type declared with a parent, or another type's parent): `ReplaceEntries`, `Mine`, `Reconcile`, `agent` / `reasons` on `Check`, `agent` on `Visible`; `Get` takes `AppResourcesGetParams` (`user`, `agent`).

## 0.1.0

- First release of `github.com/21stware/raise-go`.
- Token exchange (`client_credentials` and customer grant), per-tenant cache, refresh 60s early, single-flight.
- Generated resources: platform (whoami, ping), tables (list/get rows), membership `/me` on `ForCustomer`.
- Retries on 429 / 5xx / network errors; `Idempotency-Key` on POST; cursor iterator; typed `*Error`; webhook verify.
